# Model-series identification marks

The monochrome SVGs in this directory come unchanged from
[LobeHub Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), revision
`ea434b96e75813517b377825e01c0df61dee8836`.

Copyright (c) 2023 LobeHub. The collection is distributed under the
[MIT license](LICENSE-MIT.txt). The original source URLs, revision and SHA-256
for each file are recorded in [manifest.json](manifest.json).

ModelDock preserves the source SVG geometry. CSS masks apply the current theme
color. The marks identify OpenAI, Claude, DeepSeek, Gemini, Qwen, Meta, Grok,
Kimi, Z.ai, MiniMax, Mistral, Xiaomi MiMo, ByteDance and StepFun model families, plus the OpenRouter and
Hugging Face services.
The ByteDance company mark identifies its Seed family. Unknown families use
ModelDock's existing generic Material Symbols icon.

The names and marks remain the property of their respective owners. Their use
identifies model families and service destinations and does not imply endorsement.

The JetBrains AI Assistant service icon comes from the official
[JetBrains Brand Assets](https://www.jetbrains.com/company/brand/) product-icon
download. It is displayed as an image with its original colors and proportions.
Its separate [brand attribution and guidance](LICENSE-jetbrains-brand.txt) applies.
